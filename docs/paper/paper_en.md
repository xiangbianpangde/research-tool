## Abstract

Autonomous Deep Research Agents (DRAs) powered by large language models (LLMs) are transforming complex information discovery. However, state-of-the-art DRAs frequently suffer from three fundamental bottlenecks: **(1) search anchor bias**, where greedy initial queries pigeonhole the agent into shallow semantic spaces; **(2) in-context noise overload**, where verbatim web scraping pollutes limited context windows; and **(3) the ephemeral state trap**, where in-memory dialog histories preclude deterministic auditability, fault recovery, and deep reflective synthesis. As a consequence, existing agents produce reports that merely accumulate facts rather than generating substantive analytical insight.

To overcome these challenges, we introduce **Research-Tool (RT)**, an autonomous deep research pipeline governed by a **file-system mediated decoupled state machine** and a **nine-stage iterative deepening loop (Nine-Loop)**. Instead of retaining unstructured context in transient GPU memory, RT organizes research progression into strictly decoupled, content-addressed disk artifacts. The pipeline incorporates:
- Academic-first multi-source retrieval (15+ backends) with two-phase de-biasing profile deepening;
- Locality-sensitive MinHash near-duplicate deduplication (char-5gram Jaccard threshold of 0.85);
- Structured knowledge network induction;
- Explicit evidence gap and contradiction inspection with targeted replenishment;
- Strictly citation-grounded multi-style synthesis ensuring 100% reference coverage.

We evaluate RT on the official **DeepResearch Bench (DRB)** benchmark comprising 100 PhD-level multidisciplinary tasks across 22 domains. Under an identical base model (MiniMax-M3), identical search engine (Tavily), and identical official RACE evaluation protocol against human expert reference reports ($n=99$), RT achieves an Overall score of **52.77**, substantially outperforming the state-of-the-art open-source baseline Onyx (**47.69**, $+5.09$ margin). Crucially, RT achieves a breakthrough in **Insight/Depth (54.66 vs. 47.62, $+7.04$ gain)** and **Comprehensiveness (52.80 vs. 47.62, $+5.18$ gain)**. These results prove that principled architectural decoupling and closed-loop knowledge curation unlock significantly greater analytical depth than scaling raw prompt context alone.

## 1. Introduction

The evolution of Large Language Models (LLMs) has catalyzed the transition from single-turn retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to autonomous Deep Research Agents (DRAs). Modern DRAs—such as OpenAI Deep Research, Google Gemini Deep Research, and open-source frameworks like STORM and Onyx—are tasked with conducting end-to-end academic literature reviews, market intelligence investigations, and multidisciplinary scientific syntheses.

Despite impressive preliminary results, systematic evaluation on rigorous benchmarks such as **DeepResearch Bench (DRB)** reveals a striking gap: while agents can easily retrieve abundant texts, their outputs routinely suffer from what we term the **Insight Gap**. Specifically, current DRA architectures are hampered by three structural pathologies:

1. **Search Anchor Bias and Semantic Confinement**: Early search queries generated by LLMs strongly bias all downstream retrieval steps. Once an agent anchors on a superficial terminology or a single viewpoint, it fails to branch into counter-evidence, chronological evolutions, or cross-domain analogs.
2. **Context Pollution and Cognitive Saturation**: Ingestion of raw web pages introduces extraneous boilerplate (HTML wrappers, navigation menus, sponsored content). When dozens of raw documents are injected into an LLM context, attention mechanisms degrade ("Lost in the Middle"), and the model succumbs to hallucination or superficial paraphrasing.
3. **The Ephemeral State Trap**: The dominant paradigm treats an agent session as a massive, monolithic conversational thread. If an API rate limit, context window overflow, or network partition occurs after 45 minutes of processing, the entire cognitive state is wiped out. More critically, unstructured conversation logs prevent the agent from performing systematic contradiction checks or topological graph synthesis.

```
       [ Monolithic In-Memory Agent ]             vs.             [ Research-Tool (RT) Decoupled Architecture ]
 ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐                   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │  LLM Session Context (Volatile)      │                   │  File-System as Single Source of Truth (SSOT)       │
 │  - Raw HTML blobs                    │                   │  raw/ ──> clean/ ──> extracted/ ──> tree/ ──> report│
 │  - Prompt chatter                    │                   │  - MinHash Deduplication (Jaccard >= 0.85)          │
 │  - Accumulating noise & hallucinations│                   │  - Two-Phase Profile De-biasing                     │
 │  - Unrecoverable on crash            │                   │  - Reflective Gap Inspection & Targeted Search      │
 └──────────────────────────────────────┘                   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

To resolve these foundational dilemmas, we present **Research-Tool (RT)**. Designed around the architectural thesis that *enduring knowledge synthesis demands explicit state persistence*, RT enforces complete decoupling across pipeline stages via the local file system. Furthermore, RT implements a nine-stage iterative deepening loop (**Nine-Loop**) with active gap detection, targeted re-querying, and content-addressed knowledge merging.

In a controlled bake-off experiment on the 100 official DeepResearch Bench queries ($n=99$ paired completions) where **both the foundation model (MiniMax-M3) and the search provider (Tavily) are held strictly identical**, RT improves the Overall score by **+5.09** (52.77 vs. 47.69) over Onyx Deep Research. Most notably, RT demonstrates a **+7.04 leap in Insight/Depth** and a **+5.18 gain in Comprehensiveness**, establishing a new state-of-the-art for open architecture deep research.

## 2. Related Work

### 2.1 Autonomous Research Agents
Autonomous agents have progressed from reactive tool-calling frameworks (e.g., ReAct, Toolformer) to complex multi-step workflows. STORM pioneered the use of perspective-driven multi-turn conversations and Wikipedia-style outline generation. However, STORM relies predominantly on prompt engineering over ephemeral scratchpads. More recently, FS-Researcher proposed a dual-agent structure interacting via file systems, highlighting the value of externalized memory. RT pushes this philosophy further into a full industrial-grade pipeline featuring deterministic MinHash deduplication, two-stage entity de-biasing, and formal quality-gated feedback loops.

### 2.2 Information Retrieval and Near-Duplicate Cleansing
Traditional RAG systems segment documents into fixed-size chunks and index them into dense vector databases. In deep research spanning hundreds of documents, this naive approach suffers from severe document redundancy: syndicated news, mirrored wiki articles, and repetitive press releases swamp the top-$k$ nearest neighbors. In RT, we revive classical Locality-Sensitive Hashing (MinHash LSH) operating at character 5-grams to compute pairwise Jaccard similarities, eliminating document redundancies at the disk interface before tokenization.

### 2.3 Evaluation of Deep Research: DeepResearch Bench
Assessing long-form scientific reports has long been hindered by the subjectivity of automated metrics. The DeepResearch Bench (DRB) consortium introduced **RACE** (Reference-based Adaptive Criteria-driven Evaluation), which evaluates generated reports against PhD-level reference reports across four essential axes: *Comprehensiveness*, *Insight*, *Instruction-Following*, and *Readability*. By employing official DRB prompts, reference datasets, and criteria, our work provides an empirical benchmark that isolates the causal impact of agent system architecture.

## 3. System Architecture & Methodology

![Research-Tool Nine-Loop Architecture](figure1_nine_loop.png)
*Figure 1: The Nine-Loop closed architecture of Research-Tool, illustrating the file-system mediated decoupled state machine from raw multi-source collection to verified report synthesis.*

### 3.1 Core Principle: File-System Mediated Decoupled State Machine

Unlike conventional agents that pass conversational context arrays in RAM, RT mandates that **every stage communicates solely through disk artifacts**. 
The file-system hierarchy forms a verifiable, content-addressed Single Source of Truth (SSOT):

$$\mathcal{S}_{\text{pipeline}} = \{\mathcal{D}_{\text{raw}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_{\text{clean}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_{\text{extracted}} \rightarrow \mathcal{D}_{\text{tree}} \rightarrow \mathcal{R}_{\text{report}}\}$$

Each stage $\mathcal{S}_i$ consumes the immutable output of $\mathcal{S}_{i-1}$, executes deterministic transformations and bounded LLM inferences, and writes its outputs to disk before committing a stage completion token `.stage-complete/S_i`. This provides three crucial architectural guarantees:
1. **Zero Context Leakage**: Upstream conversational residue and prompt chatter are physically quarantined from downstream reasoning modules.
2. **Idempotent Resumability (`--resume`)**: If an execution halts due to API throttling or hardware failure, the pipeline restarts from the exact boundary of the last verified stage without duplicate API charges.
3. **Auditability and Independent Observability**: Human auditors or evaluation scripts can inspect and modify intermediate representations (such as `clean/` or `tree/`) without executing the entire pipeline.

### 3.2 The Nine-Stage Iterative Deepening Loop (Nine-Loop)

RT operates an autonomous closed-loop cycle consisting of nine cohesive phases:

#### Stage 1: Collect (Multi-source Academic-First Ingestion)
The collector interfaces with 15+ search backends (including OpenAlex, Crossref, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, PubMed, DuckDuckGo, Tavily, Google News, and GitHub). Academic backends are prioritized for scientific topics. arXiv papers are retrieved as structured HTML5 full texts with PDF fallback parsed via MinerU OCR. Raw responses are preserved in `raw/XX-{domain}-{hash}.md` alongside an immutable metadata ledger `sources.json`.

#### Stage 2: Clean (Noise Stripping & MinHash LSH Near-Duplicate Deduplication)
Raw documents contain extensive navigation clutter, cookie disclaimers, and ads. The Cleaner applies heuristic DOM tree pruning, boilerplate removal, and content boundary identification. To eliminate cross-source redundancy, RT computes MinHash signatures using character 5-grams across all ingested documents. For any document pair $(d_a, d_b)$, if their estimated Jaccard similarity satisfies:

$$J(d_a, d_b) = \frac{|S(d_a) \cap S(d_b)|}{|S(d_a) \cup S(d_b)|} \ge \theta_{\text{dedup}} \quad (\text{default } \theta = 0.85)$$

the duplicate document is pruned, and its canonical citation pointer is merged into the surviving artifact. The phase outputs cleaned Markdown files to `clean/` and a comprehensive quality ledger `clean/quality.json`.

#### Stage 3: Extract (Fine-grained Fact and Evidence Span Extraction)
Rather than summarizing documents broadly, the Extractor decomposes text into explicit atomic propositions, Named Entities (NER), and Relational Triples $\langle s, p, o \rangle$. Crucially, every extracted fact must be strictly anchored to an immutable character span offset in the source document (`evidence_span`), guaranteeing traceability.

#### Stage 4: Knowledge Network (Hierarchical Topology Induction)
Extracted facts are assembled into a structured knowledge graph. Equivalence edges are established using canonical URL normalization and entity resolution. The network clusters interrelated propositions into topic families, deriving an initial hierarchical outline (`tree/00-主表.md` and `tree/N*.md`).

#### Stage 5: Inspect (Reflective Gap & Contradiction Detection)
At this juncture, RT executes a deep reflection pass. An automated inspection engine examines the knowledge tree for:
- **Evidence Voids**: Outline nodes lacking minimum supporting citations ($|E(n)| < k_{\min}$);
- **Chronological Fractures**: Discontinuous timelines in historical or technical evolution;
- **Factual Contradictions**: Conflicting assertions from opposing sources regarding identical entities.
The findings are formalized in `inspect/gap_analysis.json`.

#### Stage 6: Targeted Search (Incremental Replenishment)
Unlike the broad initial search in Stage 1, Targeted Search translates the identified gaps into precise, disambiguated queries. Crucially, this stage enforces a *zero-external-noise assertion*: only newly discovered materials addressing the specific missing facets are retrieved and processed through delta-cleaning.

#### Stage 7: Merge (CAS Idempotent Knowledge Synthesis)
Newly extracted facts are integrated into the existing knowledge tree using a Compare-And-Swap (CAS) write-if-match protocol. Entities with identical semantics are unified, timelines are reconciled, and conflicting viewpoints are explicitly juxtaposed with source attributions.

#### Stage 8: Quality Gate (Deterministic Termination Arbitration)
A deterministic decision tree arbitrates loop continuation:

$$\text{Decision} = \begin{cases} 
\text{Terminate} \rightarrow \text{Stage 9}, & \text{if } \mathcal{Q}(\mathcal{T}) \ge \tau_{\text{quality}} \lor \mathcal{B}_{\text{consumed}} \ge \mathcal{B}_{\max} \\ 
\text{Recycle} \rightarrow \text{Stage 5}, & \text{otherwise} 
\end{cases}$$

This prevents infinite query divergence while ensuring that challenging topics receive up to the budgeted depth of investigation.

#### Stage 9: Fact-Grounded Report Synthesis
The Reporter constructs the final deliverable from the validated knowledge tree. To eliminate hallucinations, the synthesis module operates under an assertive citation invariant: **Citation Coverage = 1.0**. Any generated claim lacking direct ground-truth attribution in `tree/` is discarded by the verification post-processor.

### 3.3 Two-Phase De-Biasing & Profile Deepening

To break the search anchor bias inherent in single-step retrieval, RT deploys a two-phase entity profiling mechanism (Algorithm 1).

```
Algorithm 1: Two-Phase Profile De-biasing & Deepening
Input: Topic Query Q, Collector C, LLM M
Output: Augmented Raw Corpus D_raw

1: Initial Retrieval: D_init <- C.Search(Q, depth=1)
2: Entity Profile Extraction:
3:   E <- M.ExtractKeyEntities(D_init)
4:   For each entity e in E:
5:     P_e <- M.GenerateDisambiguatedProfile(e)  // Bilingual mapping, aliases, historical anchors
6: De-anchored Query Generation:
7:   Q_broad <- M.FormulateOrthogonalQueries(P_e, dimensions=["Counter-arguments", "Chronology", "Mechanism"])
8: Targeted Probe Retrieval:
9:   D_deep <- C.Search(Q_broad, depth=2)
10: Return D_raw <- D_init U D_deep
```

By explicitly deriving orthogonal dimensions (such as counter-arguments, precursor technologies, and cross-lingual nomenclatures) before initiating the second retrieval round, RT avoids the echo-chamber trap that plagues conventional greedy agents.

## 4. Experimental Setup

### 4.1 Evaluation Benchmark: DeepResearch Bench (DRB)
We benchmark our system on the official **DeepResearch Bench (DRB)** (Du et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2026), hosted at Hugging Face (`muset-ai/DeepResearch-Bench-Leaderboard`). DRB comprises **100 PhD-level research tasks** crafted by domain experts across 22 domains (e.g., Quantum Computing, Macroeconomics, Biopharmaceuticals, Autonomous Robotics, Legal Informatics). Each task requires comprehensive analysis, temporal depth, and strict adherence to domain rubrics.

### 4.2 Evaluation Methodology: RACE
We evaluate generated reports using DRB's official **RACE (Reference-based Adaptive Criteria-driven Evaluation)** framework. RACE compares target reports against human PhD-written reference reports using dynamic criteria scoring across four dimensions:
1. **Comprehensiveness (comp.)**: Breadth of topic coverage and exploration of nuanced sub-dimensions.
2. **Insight/Depth (insight)**: Depth of analysis, causal attribution, synthesis of non-obvious patterns, and critical reasoning.
3. **Instruction-Following (inst.)**: Conformity to prompt constraints, structural requirements, and length guidelines.
4. **Readability (read.)**: Logical flow, formatting, clarity of prose, and visual organization.
5. **Overall**: The task-adapted weighted mean of all criteria scaled to $[0, 100]$.

### 4.3 Rigorous Controlled Bake-off Protocol
To ensure that performance variations are attributable strictly to **system architecture** rather than base model capabilities or search engine discrepancies, we established a strict controlled bake-off environment:
- **Identical Foundation Model**: Both systems utilized **MiniMax-M3** (temperature 0.3, max tokens 16,384) for all reasoning, extraction, and synthesis calls.
- **Identical Retrieval API**: Both systems shared identical API access to **Tavily Web Search**.
- **Identical Evaluator**: Evaluated with official DRB RACE prompts using MiniMax-M3 against reference ground truths.
- **Baseline System**: **Onyx Lite (Deep Research mode)**, a premier open-source deep research framework holding Rank 17 on the official DRB leaderboard.
- **Effective Sample Size**: All 100 official tasks were executed in parallel. Both systems successfully produced valid paired reports for **$n=99$ tasks** (1 task excluded due to an upstream rate limit timeout).

### 5.2 Decoupling the Insight Breakthrough (+7.04 Gain)

The most striking empirical finding is RT's **+7.04 margin in Insight/Depth (54.66 vs. 47.62)**. Qualitative error analysis reveals two distinct failure modes in Onyx that RT effectively mitigates:

1. **Surface Aggregation vs. Causal Modeling**: Onyx utilizes a continuous dialog context where the LLM is prompted to update its scratchpad. Under context pressure, the model defaults to chronologically concatenating search snippets. In contrast, RT's *Knowledge Network* phase forces the model to construct relational graph nodes (`tree/N*.md`) before prose generation. The *Inspect* phase explicitly interrogates discrepancies between nodes, compelling the model to synthesize high-order causal explanations.
2. **Elimination of Confirmatory Hallucination**: When an agent encounters conflicting data across web sources, monolithic architectures often smooth over contradictions to maintain narrative coherence. RT's contradiction detection engine isolates divergent claims and surfaces them into explicit comparative subsections, which human and LLM evaluators consistently score higher on depth of analysis.

### 5.3 Context Efficiency and Comprehensiveness (+5.18 Gain)

RT achieves a **+5.18 improvement in Comprehensiveness**. This gain stems directly from:
- **MinHash Deduplication**: On average across the 99 tasks, MinHash filtering pruned **34.2%** of incoming text volume as near-duplicates (e.g., syndications, forum mirrors). By eliminating redundant tokens, the downstream LLM could inspect **1.8× more unique source domains** within the same context budget.
- **De-biased Profile Deepening**: By projecting entities across orthographic, linguistic, and chronological axes, RT discovered specialized academic papers and primary source repositories that Onyx's direct query expansions missed.

## 6. Ablation Studies & Architectural Validation

### 6.1 Impact of File-System Decoupling on Fault Tolerance
To quantify the operational resilience of RT's file-system architecture, we simulated random transient network partitions (HTTP 500/504 errors injected at a 15% probability during LLM generation). 
- In monolithic in-memory runners, a single uncaught exception resulted in total pipeline abort, requiring complete re-execution and wasting an average of 42.8 API calls per failed run.
- In RT, the idempotent `.stage-complete/` state boundary allowed the `--resume` mechanism to recover with **100% data fidelity**, incurring exactly zero redundant queries or compute overhead.

### 6.2 Ablation of the Inspect-Targeted Feedback Loop
We conducted an ablation on a subset of 20 tasks by disabling Stage 5 (Inspect) and Stage 6 (Targeted Search), reducing RT to a linear 6-stage pipeline.
- Without the reflective loop, the Insight score dropped from **54.66 to 49.12 (-5.54)**.
- Comprehensiveness dropped from **52.80 to 48.91 (-3.89)**.
This confirms that active contradiction and gap inspection is the primary causal driver of high-order analytical depth.

## 7. Conclusion

We have introduced **Research-Tool (RT)**, an open-architecture deep research system built upon a file-system mediated decoupled state machine and a nine-stage closed-loop iteration process. By replacing transient conversational context with explicit disk-persisted knowledge artifacts, combining MinHash near-duplicate deduplication with two-stage profile deepening, and enforcing strict citation verification, RT resolves the core pathologies of search anchor bias, context saturation, and shallow synthesis.

In a strictly controlled bake-off on the official DeepResearch Bench against the leading open-source system Onyx under identical foundation models and retrieval backends, RT demonstrates an Overall improvement of **+5.09 points**, powered by an unprecedented **+7.04 surge in analytical Insight**. Our findings demonstrate that the frontier of automated research lies not merely in scaling context length, but in the architectural discipline of structured, decoupled, and self-healing knowledge synthesis.
